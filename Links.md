## Project Summary

LeadLoop AI is an intelligent lead intake and sales automation platform that transforms incoming customer enquiries into structured, actionable sales opportunities.

It uses an AI Agent to understand enquiries, qualify leads, detect duplicates, route uncertain cases through human review, assign sales representatives, create follow-up tasks, and maintain an auditable automation workflow.
